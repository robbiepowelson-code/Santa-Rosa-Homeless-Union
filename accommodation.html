Disability Accommodation Helper

Santa Rosa Homeless Union — help a fellow member ask for the accommodations the law gives them

How this works: answer the questions below in plain words, press the red button, and this page writes a formal accommodation request letter and a permission slip (release). Print it, save it as a PDF, or email it. Nothing you type is sent anywhere — it stays on this device. This is a self-advocacy tool, not legal advice.

1. Who needs the accommodation?

2. What is their situation at Sam Jones Hall right now?

3. Describe the disability in their own words

Plain words are fine: "I had a stroke," "I have ADHD and forget rules," "I'm having surgery and will be on crutches," "I hear voices when stressed." Do NOT attach medical records — they are never required at this stage.

4. What happened, or what is about to happen? (optional)

If they got a violation, were kicked out, or something is coming up (like a surgery), say what happened in a few sentences. Stick to facts they know are true.

5. What accommodations do they need?

Check everything that applies. The first two are part of every Union request.

6. Who is helping them? (the Union representative)

Read it out loud together. Fix anything that isn't right (you can edit the text after printing, or change your answers above and press the button again). Then have the member sign the last page — pen, or a finger/typed signature on a PDF both count. Email it to the shelter director and keep a copy.
